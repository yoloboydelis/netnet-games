How the real-pot version will work COMING SOON

  • Entry in wsNET: each attempt costs a small wsNET fee.
  • Pot grows: most of every fee goes into the jackpot pot; it keeps growing until someone beats the record.
  • Beat the record to win: only a score above the current high score takes the pot. Each new record becomes the next target.
  • Treasury share: a share of each fee funds the NetNet Capital treasury, and a reserve re-seeds the next pot.
  • Skill only: seeded food, no random drops, no luck.
  • Replay-verified: winning runs are re-simulated from their input log before payout. No API-call leaderboards.

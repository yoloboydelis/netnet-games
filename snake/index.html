HOW SCORES ARE VERIFIED

Everyone gets the same level this week (seed from the UTC week). Your inputs are recorded tick by tick; at game over the shared verifier re-plays them from scratch and only the re-simulated score counts. Runs that go faster than real time, pause, hide the tab, or exceed the input-rate cap are rejected.

How to play

  1. Same tape for everyone. Each day (00:00 UTC) a new 24-second tape drops: 6 seconds each of tokenized gold, NVIDIA, a 10Y Treasury bond and real estate. Simulated price action from a public seed.
  2. Hold LONG to ride it up, hold SHORT to ride it down, let go to park in T-bills (small yield). 3× leverage.
  3. Learn the tape. Gold and NVIDIA get dips that bounce back. The bond gaps down on a hot CPI print. Real estate pays rent every 1.5 s. Practice is free and unlimited.
  4. Don't get liquidated. Equity under 50% = 💀 game over. Every switch costs a 0.15% fee, with a 0.2 s cooldown, so spamming doesn't pay.
  5. Share your run. Your challenge link carries your replay, so friends play the same tape and the score is re-checked in their browser.

Rules & economics (real version, not live)

  • Free practice: unlimited runs on today's tape, always free. You pay only to submit a ranked run.
  • Ranked entry: 1 USDG (or the equivalent in wsNET) per ranked run. Your best ranked run of the day counts.
  • Split, taken at entry: 70% daily prize pool · 25% NetNet treasury · 5% seed for tomorrow's pool. The treasury takes its cut first and never puts in its own money.
  • Payout at 00:00 UTC: top 10% of ranked runs. 1st 30%, 2nd 18%, 3rd 12%, 4th–5th 6%, 6th–8th 4%, 9th–10th 3%; the last 10% is split across the rest of the top 10%.
  • Duels: send a challenge link with a stake. Both play the same tape; higher score takes 90%, 10% goes to the treasury.
  • Ties: the earlier submission wins. No random draws, ever. Winning runs are re-simulated from their input log before payout.

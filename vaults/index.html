Risks, plainly

  • You can lose money versus just holding. In our own simulation the vaults ended below "just hold" in most scenarios once impermanent loss is counted.
  • Tight ranges are dangerous on volatile tokens. On a NET-like 90% volatility path, ±5–10% ranges lost most of their value. The plan uses wide, volatility-scaled ranges.
  • Zero-swap is not magic. It cuts swap costs about 95%, but in strong trends it ends up holding the losing token.
  • Smart-contract risk. Two audits, a bug bounty through NetNet Prizes, deposit caps, a 48h timelock and a multisig are planned. Withdrawals can never be paused.
  • Tokenized-stock and stablecoin risk. Issuer, depeg and market-hours gaps.
  • Regulatory. MiCA/MiFID review is needed before any EU launch. Not available to anyone yet.

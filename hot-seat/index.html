How it works

  1. Take the seat. The first take of a round costs $1 USDG. Every take after that costs 2% more than the one before.
  2. The clock resets. Every take resets the countdown to 60 seconds. A take in the last 10 seconds gets +15 seconds on top (75s total), so sniping the final second doesn't pay off.
  3. No back-to-back. The current holder can't take the seat again. Someone else has to take it first.
  4. Split of every take: 82% pot · 10% NetNet treasury (USDG) · 4% NET buyback · 3% Grand Slam reserve · 1% ops.
  5. Clock hits zero: the holder wins 75% of the pot. The other 25% seeds the next round, so a pot never starts at zero.
  6. Grand Slam: every 25th round (fixed, never random) starts with the whole Grand Slam reserve added to its pot.
  7. No randomness anywhere. No hidden explosion odds, no random bonus. The result depends only on when players choose to take the seat.

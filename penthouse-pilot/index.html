PENTHOUSE PILOT

Tap anywhere on the game (or press Space / ↑) to flap. Each tap gives aidunlap's jetpack one burst upward; gravity does the rest. Fly through the gaps: each tower +10, tokens in the gap T-bill +20, gold +30, NET +50. Hit a tower or the street and the flight ends. Tap again to fly instantly.

Ranked entry: 100 test credits (10 to treasury first, 90 to this week's pool). Untick Ranked for free practice.

ABOUT THIS CABINET

An original NetNet game starring aidunlap, the NetNet mascot. Everyone gets the same level this week (seed from the UTC week, Sunday to Sunday). Every flap is recorded by tick and the score server re-plays the run from scratch; only the re-simulated score counts. Runs that go faster than real time, pause, hide the tab or exceed the tap-rate cap are rejected. Tokens in the game are game items, not real assets or prices. Test credits only, not money.

HOW APPS MAKE MONEY FOR NET

Every app follows the arcade rule: the treasury cut comes first, pools and rewards are funded by users or sponsors, never by the treasury's own capital. Revenue lines: sponsor pools (Voices), service fees in wsNET (Launch Kit), fees on deposits (Savings Goals, Vaults) and entries (Arcade). Today everything runs on TEST CREDITS.

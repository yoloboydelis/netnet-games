How it works

Like a fantasy league, but for people who read earnings reports.

1

Pick & weight

Choose 1–5 assets and set weights in 5% steps (5% min, 60% max per asset). Lock in before Monday's open.

2

The week plays out

Buy-and-hold for the trading week. Your score uses public closing prices from the onchain price feed, the same for everyone.

3

Ranked & paid

Friday close: portfolios ranked by score. Top 10% are paid in USDG on a tiered scale, settled onchain.

Rules

  • Score = weekly return % − 0.5 × max drawdown %. Return counts, but so does the pain along the way.
  • Ties: lower max drawdown wins, then earliest lock-in. No random draws, ever.
  • Entry split: 75% prize pool · 20% NetNet treasury · 5% next-season seed. Fixed, published, onchain.
  • Payout: top 10% of entrants. 1st 25%, 2nd 15%, 3rd 10%, 4th–10th 4% each, the remaining 22% split equally across the rest of the top 10%.
  • Prizes come only from entries, so the treasury never risks its own capital. The 5% seed tops up next season's pool and funds free Standard entries for top Paper League players.
  • One entry per verified account per league. Picks are hashed onchain at lock-in and can't change.
